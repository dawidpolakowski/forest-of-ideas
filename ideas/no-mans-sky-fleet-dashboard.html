No Man's Sky – Unified Fleet Dashboard

Games · No Man's Sky

A comprehensive dashboard for managing, deploying, and monitoring your entire fleet across multiple star systems in real-time.

Problem

Fleet management in No Man's Sky is fragmented and tedious. Players must manually travel to each frigate, check individual status panels, and piece together fleet data across multiple menus. There's no central overview of fleet composition, missions, cargo, or performance metrics. This creates friction for players who want to optimize their fleet operations and reduces engagement with fleet mechanics.

Proposed Solution: Fleet Dashboard Core

  • Central dashboard showing all frigates with real-time status, health, and morale indicators
  • Mission queue with visual progress tracking and estimated completion times
  • Fleet cargo summary with resource breakdown and storage optimization suggestions
  • Frigate deployment simulator to predict mission outcomes before committing
  • Fleet analytics: historical mission success rates, cost-benefit analysis, crew efficiency metrics
  • Quick-deploy feature for repeated missions with one-click deployment
  • Remote ship management: transfer crew, repair, and upgrade frigates from the dashboard

AI Crew System

Recruit and manage autonomous AI crew members who can execute specialized missions independently.

  • AI Character Recruitment: Hire AI crew members from space stations, trading posts, or rescue missions. Each character has unique traits, skills, and specializations
  • Character Specializations:
    • Miners – Extract resources from planets and asteroids
    • Explorers – Scout new systems and catalog discoveries
    • Traders – Buy/sell resources and negotiate better prices
    • Engineers – Repair damaged equipment and craft items
    • Scientists – Research alien technology and upload data
  • Task Assignment: Send AI crew on missions via the dashboard:
    • Resource collection (mining, farming, gas harvesting)
    • Planetary exploration with photo evidence and data gathering
    • Building and base construction assistance
    • Trade runs between bases and space stations
    • Alien artifact collection and research
  • Real-Time Tracking: Monitor crew on active missions:
    • Live location and progress updates on galactic map
    • Resource collection counter and efficiency metrics
    • Estimated completion time and return schedule
    • Emergency recall option for urgent situations
  • Auto-Return Protocol: Crew automatically returns to designated base when:
    • Task completes (inventory full or objective reached)
    • Health/condition drops below threshold
    • Time limit expires
    • Player manually triggers return command
  • Crew Management Interface:
    • Character profiles showing experience, skill levels, and specializations
    • Work history and mission success rates
    • Morale and satisfaction tracking (affects efficiency and retention)
    • Leveling system – crew becomes more efficient with experience
    • Equipment upgrades to enhance crew capabilities
  • Crew Economics:
    • Pay crew members to maintain morale and prevent attrition
    • Resources collected partially returned to player (rest covers crew costs)
    • Long-term ROI tracking – see profitability of each crew member

Impact

Transforms passive fleet management into an active, engaging meta-game. The unified dashboard eliminates tedious menu navigation, while the AI crew system creates a whole new layer of gameplay—players manage and nurture a team of autonomous workers performing tasks in parallel. This dramatically multiplies passive income generation, enables diverse playstyles (focus on exploration, mining, trading, or building), and creates compelling character progression separate from the main player character. Players feel less alone in the galaxy with a crew of personalities to manage and relationships to develop. Increased engagement, longer session times, and stronger player investment in long-term progression.
