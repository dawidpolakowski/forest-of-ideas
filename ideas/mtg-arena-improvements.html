MTG Arena – Deck Simulation Mode

Games · MTG

Add an advanced deck testing sandbox with AI simulation and probability insights for faster optimization and deeper strategic gameplay.

Problem

Deck testing in MTG Arena is slow and lacks analytical tools. Players cannot simulate multiple hands or understand draw probabilities efficiently. This creates a barrier to deck optimization and forces players to rely on intuition rather than data.

Proposed Solution

  • Simulate thousands of opening hands instantly with statistical analysis
  • Display probability curves for drawing key cards in any turn
  • AI opponent simulation with multiple difficulty profiles (aggro/control/combo)
  • Goldfish mode for solo testing and performance baseline tracking
  • Export simulation data as detailed reports for sharing with deck communities

Impact

Empowers players with data-driven deck building, reduces iteration time, and elevates competitive gameplay. Creates opportunity for deeper strategic analysis and community discussion around optimal deck compositions.
