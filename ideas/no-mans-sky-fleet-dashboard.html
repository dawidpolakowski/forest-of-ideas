No Man's Sky – Unified Fleet Dashboard

A comprehensive dashboard for managing, deploying, and monitoring your entire fleet across multiple star systems in real-time.

Problem

Fleet management in No Man's Sky is fragmented and tedious. Players must manually travel to each frigate, check individual status panels, and piece together fleet data across multiple menus. There's no central overview of fleet composition, missions, cargo, or performance metrics. This creates friction for players who want to optimize their fleet operations and reduces engagement with fleet mechanics.

Proposed Solution

  • Central dashboard showing all frigates with real-time status, health, and morale indicators
  • Mission queue with visual progress tracking and estimated completion times
  • Fleet cargo summary with resource breakdown and storage optimization suggestions
  • Frigate deployment simulator to predict mission outcomes before committing
  • Fleet analytics: historical mission success rates, cost-benefit analysis, crew efficiency metrics
  • Quick-deploy feature for repeated missions with one-click deployment
  • Remote ship management: transfer crew, repair, and upgrade frigates from the dashboard

Impact

Elevates fleet management from a tedious chore to a engaging meta-game. Players gain deeper strategic control, reduce micromanagement friction, and have tools to optimize their fleet for specific playstyles. Increased fleet engagement drives longer session times and retention.
