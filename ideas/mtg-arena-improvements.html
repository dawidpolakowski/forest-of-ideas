MTG Arena – Deck Simulation Mode

Add an advanced deck testing sandbox with AI simulation and probability insights.

Problem

Deck testing in MTG Arena is slow and lacks analytical tools. Players cannot simulate multiple hands or understand draw probabilities efficiently.

Proposed Solution

  • Simulate thousands of opening hands instantly
  • Display probability of drawing key cards
  • AI opponent simulation (aggro/control)
  • Goldfish mode for solo testing

Impact

Faster deck optimization and deeper strategic gameplay.
